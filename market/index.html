📰 Market note — September 2026

  • HDB rents dipped 0.6% from August but stayed 1.1% higher than a year earlier.
  • By flat type, year on year: 3-room +0.9%, 4-room +0.9%, 5-room +1.0%, Executive +3.5%.
  • About 2,379 HDB flats were rented in September — down 15.4% from August and 9.3% below a year ago, so fewer flats are changing hands.
  • Condo rents were flat (−0.1% month on month) and 2.4% higher than a year ago; about 6,774 condos were rented.
  • What it means for room renters: rents are rising only slowly (about 1% a year for HDB), so there is little reason to accept a big increase at renewal — ask for a fair price.

No major site publishes monthly room-only rents yet. RoomKaki will add real room prices from posts on our own site as they come in.
