# RoomKaki — Launch & Boost Plan (no-cost first, tiny paid second)
*2026-07-13. Companion to MASTER_PLAN.md. Rule: every dollar must beat the free
alternative before it's spent.*

## Stage A — Prototype feedback (this week, $0)
1. Publish prototype to github.io (pending user phrase) → share in 2–3 trusted
   Burmese groups as "I'm building this for us — what's missing?" NOT a launch;
   an invitation. Feedback = free market research + first believers.
2. Founding-member list: simple Google Form (name + WhatsApp + "renter/landlord")
   linked from the prototype banner. Target: 100 sign-ups pre-launch.
3. User's personal network: recruit the promised 30–50 concierge listings.

## Stage B — Organic engine ($0/month, runs on existing assets)
| Channel | What | Cadence | Why it's free |
|---|---|---|---|
| Video factory | Burmese "rent safely in SG" shorts (scam stories, HDB rules, deposit checklist) each ending with roomkaki.com | 1/week | Pipeline already built (viral-shorts-factory) |
| TikTok (logged in) | Same shorts reposted + listing-tour clips | 2–3/week | Repurposed content |
| Facebook page + groups | RoomKaki page; weekly "new rooms this week" digest posted where admins allow; NEVER spam | 1–2/week | Community-first etiquette |
| Telegram | RoomKaki channel mirroring the digest | weekly | Burmese community lives here |
| Partnerships | 2 associations/temples + 3 Peninsula Plaza shops carry a QR poster | one-time | Print cost ~S$20 total |
| WhatsApp referral | "Invite 3 friends → founding-member badge + free featured listing" | built-in | Costs a badge |
| SEO area pages | /rooms-near-boon-lay etc. (post-MVP; compounds over 6–12 months) | auto-generated | Static pages |

## Stage C — Paid (ONLY after Gate G1, hard-capped)
Budget cap: **S$100/month**, kill rule: stop any channel where a registered user
costs > S$2.
1. **Boost the winner, don't buy cold ads**: take the best-performing organic FB
   post, boost S$5/day × 7 days targeting Burmese-language users in Singapore.
2. TikTok Promote on best listing-tour clip: S$20 test once.
3. NOT doing: Google Ads (wrong intent + expensive for a community marketplace),
   influencer payments (community trust can't be bought), app-install campaigns
   (no app).
Order of spend: FB boost → measure → TikTok → measure. Anything unspent stays unspent.

## Sequencing (matches classifier + user intent: plan BEFORE public launch)
prototype public (feedback only) → MVP build → private beta w/ seeds →
**G1 passed** → public launch posts + posters + paid tests.
The github.io prototype is NOT the launch — it's the feedback tool.
