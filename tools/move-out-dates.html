📅 Move-out & deposit dates

Your last day from the notice period, and when your deposit is due.

Singapore law does not set one notice period for rooms — your agreement does. HDB rentals must last at least 6 months. If a date falls on a weekend or public holiday, agree the handover day with your landlord.
