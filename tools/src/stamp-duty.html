Formula from IRAS (iras.gov.sg, "Renting a property"): for a lease of 4 years or less, stamp duty is 0.4% of the total rent for the whole lease, rounded down to the dollar, minimum S$1. No duty if the average yearly rent is S$1,000 or less. Leases longer than 4 years use 0.4% of 4 years' average rent. Stamp the agreement within 14 days of signing (30 days if signed overseas) on iras.gov.sg. Checked November 2026.
