Rules from hdb.gov.sg ("Regulations for renting out bedrooms / flat"), checked November 2026: only 3-room or bigger flats may rent out bedrooms; the owner must keep living in the flat; minimum 6 months per tenant; maximum 6 people in a 3-room flat; maximum 8 people in a 4-room or bigger flat for rentals ending by 31 Dec 2028 (then 6). Whole-flat rental has extra conditions (e.g. owner's minimum occupation period, non-citizen quota). This check is a guide — HDB decides.
