HDB room rental rules: minimum stay, occupancy and what is legal

2026-11-19 · RoomKaki guides

Most affordable rooms in Singapore are inside HDB flats, and HDB has rules that protect you — if you know them.

Minimum 6 months

HDB rooms cannot legally be rented for less than 6 months. If someone offers an HDB room for "2–3 months, short term", that tenancy is not allowed, and if HDB finds out, you can be asked to leave with little recourse. Private condos are different: the legal minimum there is 3 months.

The flat has an occupancy cap

An HDB flat has a maximum number of occupants (for example, a 4-room flat is capped at 6 unrelated persons). If a flat is packed beyond the cap with bed-spaces, the whole arrangement is illegal — and everyone in it is at risk, not just the landlord.

The landlord must register you

Flat owners must register every tenant with HDB. A landlord who refuses to register you is hiding something — often an over-capacity flat or an owner who is not allowed to rent out at all. You can politely ask: "Will you register my stay with HDB?" The answer tells you a lot.

Your immigration status matters to the landlord too

Owners may rent only to people legally in Singapore (work pass, student pass, PR, etc.), and pass validity is checked at registration. Have your pass details ready — a serious landlord will ask, and that is a good sign, not a bad one.

What this means for you

  • Avoid "short-term HDB" offers — they are outside the rules from day one.
  • Ask how many people live in the flat before you commit.
  • Ask whether you will be registered with HDB. The answer should be yes, immediately.

Rules summarised as of November 2026 — always check the current version at hdb.gov.sg.
