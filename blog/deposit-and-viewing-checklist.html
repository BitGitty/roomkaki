Deposits and viewings: what to check before you pay

2026-11-20 · RoomKaki guides

The one rule first: never transfer any money before you have stood inside the room. Almost every rental loss we hear about broke this rule.

What deposit is normal

  • Security deposit: usually 1 month's rent for a 12-month stay (sometimes half a month for 6 months).
  • Advance rent: the first month, paid when you sign — not weeks before moving in.
  • Anything beyond that ("booking fee", "admin fee", "insurance") deserves hard questions.

At the viewing, check these 10 things

  • Water pressure and heater in the bathroom you will use
  • Aircon: turn it on, listen, smell — ask who pays for servicing
  • Phone signal inside the room
  • Window: does it open, is there daylight, any noise from the road
  • Mattress and furniture condition (photograph everything)
  • Washing machine access and rules
  • Cooking: actually allowed, or "light cooking" meaning none?
  • Who else lives there — how many people share the bathroom
  • Locks: does the room door lock, who else keeps a key
  • Agreed house rules: visitors, overnight guests, aircon hours

Before you hand over the deposit

  • Get a written agreement — names, room, rent, deposit, notice period, what the deposit may be deducted for.
  • Photograph the room's condition on day one and send the photos to the landlord in chat, so the record has a date both sides can see.
  • Pay traceably (bank transfer with reference), never cash without a signed receipt.

A deposit is YOUR money held in trust. A clear paper trail is what gets it back.
